The model is a prioritization layer. A flagged SKU should trigger investigation of demand, lifecycle, purchasing commitments and reserve policy—not an automatic accounting action.
01 / RECENCY

Days since last sale

A direct indicator of demand inactivity. The notebook also creates a 90-day no-sales flag for business interpretation.

02 / COVER

Months of cover

Current stock divided by average monthly sales. High cover can indicate inventory that is out of proportion to demand.

03 / AGEING

180+ day stock ratio

Measures the share of inventory that has aged beyond 180 days and may require closer review.

04 / VELOCITY

Sales-to-stock ratio

Low sales relative to stock can reveal slow-moving or potentially trapped working capital.

Model architecture

Isolation Forest review prioritization

  • 500 synthetic SKUs across Metal, Vinyl, Windows, Composite and OPP.
  • Four model features: days since last sale, months of cover, 180+ day inventory ratio and sales-to-stock ratio.
  • Inputs standardized with StandardScaler.
  • IsolationForest with 100 estimators, contamination = 0.05 and random_state = 42.
  • 25 SKUs were flagged as anomalies in the stored notebook output.
Governance boundary

Model flag ≠ business disposition

The application is intentionally framed as a review queue. A flagged item can reflect stale demand, excess stock, ageing, unusual velocity or a combination. Finance, Supply Chain and product owners still need to validate the commercial context before reserve, purchasing or write-off decisions.

Portfolio boundary: synthetic data only. No employer, customer, SKU, reserve, sales or inventory records are included.
